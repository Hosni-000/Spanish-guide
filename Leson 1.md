# 🇪🇸 Spanish — Lesson 01

## Gender, Articles & Everyday Expressions

> [!tip] Core idea Spanish is heavily based on **agreement**. Words change depending on whether the noun is **masculine/feminine** and **singular/plural**.

---

## 1. `como` vs `cómo`

### `como`

**I eat** — from `comer` (to eat)

> **Como todas las mañanas.**  
> I eat every morning.

### `cómo`

**How**

> **¿Cómo estás?**  
> How are you?

> [!warning] Accent matters `como` ≠ `cómo`
> 
> - **como** → I eat
> - **cómo** → how

Context helps you distinguish them.

---

# 2. "Every" — `todo/toda/todos/todas`

Spanish commonly expresses **every + noun** using:

> **todo/toda/todos/todas + definite article + noun**

The words must agree with the noun.

|Spanish|Meaning|Why?|
|---|---|---|
|**todo el día**|every day / all day|`día` = masculine singular|
|**toda la noche**|every night / all night|`noche` = feminine singular|
|**todos los días**|every day|`día` = masculine plural|
|**todas las noches**|every night|`noche` = feminine plural|
|**todas las mañanas**|every morning|`mañana` = feminine plural|

### Pattern

**Masculine singular**

> todo + el + noun

**Feminine singular**

> toda + la + noun

**Masculine plural**

> todos + los + noun

**Feminine plural**

> todas + las + noun

# 3. Useful expressions

### `todas las mañanas`

**every morning**

> **Como todas las mañanas.**  
> I eat every morning.

Breakdown:

- `como` → I eat
- `todas` → all/every
- `las` → the
- `mañanas` → mornings

Literally, it resembles **"all the mornings"**, but naturally it means **"every morning."**

---

### `todos los días`

**every day**

> **Como todos los días.**  
> I eat every day.

---

### `todas las noches`

**every night**

> **Como todas las noches.**  
> I eat every night.

---

# 4. `en casa` vs `desde casa`

These are worth learning as a pair.

### `en casa`

**at home**

> **Como en casa.**  
> I eat at home.

`en` = in / at

---

### `desde casa`

**from home**

> **Trabajo desde casa.**  
> I work from home.

`desde` = from / since

So:

> **Trabajo en casa.**  
> I work **at home**.

> **Trabajo desde casa.**  
> I work **from home**.

---

# 5. Sentence breakdown

> **Como en casa todas las noches.**

|Word|Meaning|
|---|---|
|`como`|I eat|
|`en`|at/in|
|`casa`|home/house|
|`todas las noches`|every night|

Natural translation:

> **I eat at home every night.**

---

> **Trabajo desde casa todos los días.**

|Word|Meaning|
|---|---|
|`trabajo`|I work|
|`desde`|from|
|`casa`|home|
|`todos los días`|every day|

Natural translation:

> **I work from home every day.**

# 🧠 What I already know

As an Arabic speaker, grammatical gender and agreement should feel relatively familiar.

Spanish has:

- masculine ↔ feminine
- singular ↔ plural
- adjective/article agreement
- gendered articles

For example:

> `todos los días`  
> masculine + plural

> `todas las noches`  
> feminine + plural

Don't memorize these as completely unrelated phrases. Learn the **agreement pattern**.
