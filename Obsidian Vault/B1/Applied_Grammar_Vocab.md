# B1 — Applied Grammar + Vocabulary Cards

? Apply Present Subjunctive + B1 environment/work vocabulary: "It's important that we take care of the environment; it's possible that pollution increases."?
Answer: Es importante que cuidemos el medio ambiente. Es posible que la contaminación aumente. | Grammar: present subjunctive in noun clauses (cuidemos — regular; aumente — irregular stem? aumentar is regular → aumente); trigger words (es importante que, es posible que).

? Apply Imperfect Subjunctive + work/opinion: "If I had a job, I would work more; I wanted them to approve the project."?
Answer: Si tuviera un empleo, trabajaría más. Quería que aprobaran el proyecto. | Grammar: imperfect subjunctive (tuviera, aprobara) in hypothetical/if clauses and after past tense triggers; conditional (trabajaría) paired with imperfect subjunctive.

? Apply Relative Pronouns + B1 vocabulary: "The employee who works here is responsible; the company whose manager is strict is efficient."?
Answer: El empleado que trabaja aquí es responsable. La empresa cuyo gerente es estricto es eficiente. | Grammar: relative pronoun que (for people/things); cuyo (possessive, agrees with possessed noun — gerente, empresa? Wait: cuyo gerente — gerente is possessed, company is owner, cuyo agrees with possessed noun = gerente (masculine singular) — correct).

? Apply Future/Conditional + media/opinion: "If the climate changes, society will suffer; we will read the news."?
Answer: Si el clima cambia, la sociedad sufrirá. Leeremos las noticias. | Grammar: future simple (sufrirá, leeremos); conditional paired with present in real conditions (si presente → futuro) and with imperfect subjunctive in hypothetical conditions.

? Apply Verbal Periphrases + B1 routine: "I've been working for ten years; I just finished my report."?
Answer: Llevo diez años trabajando. Acabo de terminar mi informe. | Grammar: llevar + gerundio (duration); acabar de + infinitive (just completed).
