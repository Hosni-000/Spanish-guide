# B1 — Future Simple & Conditional Tenses

? How is future simple of regular verbs formed?
Answer: Infinitive + endings: -é, -ás, -á, -emos, -éis, -án (no stem change except irregular stems like dir-, pondr-, etc.).
Examples: Cantará mañana. / Nosotros comeremos afuera.
Pitfall: Future replaces the infinitive suffix with future endings; stem changes for irregulars.

:: Give future of irregular verbs: SER, IR, VENIR, TENER, SABER, HABER, PODER, PONER, QUERER, DECIR, HACER, DAR. :: Seré, iré, vendré, tendré, sabré, habré, podré, pondré, querré, diré, haré, daré.
Examples: Yo iré al cine mañana. / Ella tendrá veinte años.
Pitfall: The stem for irregular future is often different from present; memorize by list.

:: How is Conditional formed? :: Infinitive + endings: -ía, -ías, -ía, -íamos, -íais, -ían. Same irregular stems as future.
Examples: Yo comería si tuviera hambre. / Si lloviera, no saldríamos.
Pitfall: Conditional expresses hypothetical/probability: "If I had time, I would travel."
