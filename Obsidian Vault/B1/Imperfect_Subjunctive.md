# B1 — Imperfect Subjunctive (Formations & Uses)

:: How is imperfect subjunctive formed? :: Take the 3rd person plural (ellos) preterite form, drop -on, then add -a, -as, -a, -amos, -ais, -an.
Examples: Hicieron → hiciera / Hicieras / Hiciera / Hiciéramos / Hicierais / Hicieran. Vivieron → viviera / vivieras.
Pitfall: Do not confuse with imperfect indicative; imperfect subjunctive always has these endings and no accents except on stressed syllable as rules apply.

? When is imperfect subjunctive used?
Answer: In dependent clauses when the main clause is in past tense and requires subjunctive; also in "si" clauses describing hypothetical/unreal conditions (si tuviera...); and for politeness (quisiera... instead of quiero... in formal speech).
Examples: Si tuviera dinero, viajaría. / Quería que él viniera. / Aunque fuera tarde, salimos.
Pitfall: Remember it pairs with conditional in hypothetical present/unreal past conditions (Type 2/3 conditions).
