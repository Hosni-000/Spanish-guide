# B1 — Relative Clauses (Restrictive vs. Non-Restrictive)

:: What's the difference between restrictive and non-restrictive relative clauses? :: Restrictive defines noun and limits meaning: El libro que leo es interesante (only that one). Non-restrictive adds extra info: El libro, que es interesante, lo compré ayer.
Examples: Las personas que viven cerca son amables (restrictive). / Mi hermano, que vive en Madrid, es médico (non-restrictive - uses commas and indicative only; if dependent clause requires subjunctive then subjunctive applies regardless).
Pitfall: Non-restrictive clauses require commas and often indicative; do not confuse with subjunctive triggers.
