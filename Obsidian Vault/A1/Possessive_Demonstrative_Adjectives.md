# A1 — Possessive & Demonstrative Adjectives

:: What are the possessive adjectives for singular and plural? :: Mi / tu / su / nuestro / vuestro / su — plural: mis / tus / sus / nuestros / vuestros / sus.
Examples: Mi casa es grande. / Nuestros amigos vienen mañana.
Pitfall: Nuestro/nuestra agree in gender and number with the noun possessed, not the owner.

? How do demonstrative adjectives agree?
Answer: Este/esta/estos/estas (this/these — near); ese/esa/esos/esas (that/those — medium); aquel/aquella/aquellos/aquellas (that over there — far).
Examples: Este libro es mío. / Esos coches son nuevos.
Pitfall: Este/esta agree with the noun; do not translate literally from English.

:: Give example showing agreement of possessive with possessed noun, not owner gender. :: Nuestra abuela (our grandmother — our is masculine/feminine based on abuela being feminine).
Examples: Nuestro hermano (masculine). / Nuestras hermanas (feminine plural).
Pitfall: If you say "nuestro hermana", you are wrong — agree with hermana (feminine).
