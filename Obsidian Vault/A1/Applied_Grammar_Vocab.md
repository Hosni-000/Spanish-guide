# A1 — Applied Grammar + Vocabulary Cards

? Apply present of SER with family/identity vocabulary: "My father is from Mexico; my mother is a doctor; we are students."?
Answer: Mi padre es de México; mi madre es médica; nosotros somos estudiantes. | Grammar: ser = identity/origin/profession; present conjugation; gender agreement with profession (médica for female).

? Apply present of ESTAR with location/state vocabulary: "The book is on the table; I am tired; the water is cold."?
Answer: El libro está en la mesa. Yo estoy cansado. El agua está fría. | Grammar: estar = location/state/temporary condition; agreement with adjectives (fría for feminine agua).

? Apply regular -ar verbs with A1 nouns/objects: "We study at school; she walks to the park; they work in the store."?
Answer: Nosotros estudiamos en la escuela. Ella camina al parque. Ellos trabajan en la tienda. | Grammar: regular -ar conjugation; preposition a for movement toward.

? Apply gender/article agreement with color/size vocabulary: "The red apple is small; the big house is blue."?
Answer: La manzana roja es pequeña. La casa grande es azul. | Grammar: adjective placement; gender agreement (manzana/feminine; casa/feminine); number agreement.
