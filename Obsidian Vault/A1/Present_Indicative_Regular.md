# A1 — Present Indicative (Regular -ar, -er, -ir)

? How do regular -ar verbs conjugate in the present indicative first person singular?
Answer: Replace -ar with -o (hablo, trabajo, estudio).
Examples: Yo hablo español. / Nosotros trabajamos juntos.
Pitfall: The -o ending is first person singular (yo); do not confuse with -a which is third person singular.

? What are the six present indicative endings for regular -er verbs?
Answer: -o, -es, -e, -emos, -éis, -en.
Examples: Yo como manzanas. / Ellos beben agua.
Pitfall: The vosotros form -éis is used in Spain; Latin America uses -en for ustedes.

? How do regular -ir verbs differ from -er verbs in the present?
Answer: They share -o, -es, -e, -imos, -ís, -en — only nosotros/vosotros use -imos/-ís instead of -emos/-éis.
Examples: Yo vivo en Madrid. / Nosotros escribimos cartas.
Pitfall: -ir and -er are identical except in the 1st and 2nd plural (nosotros/vosotros).

:: Conjugate "estudiar" fully in present indicative (yo / tú / él / nosotros / vosotros / ellos). :: Yo estudio / tú estudias / él estudia / nosotros estudiamos / vosotros estudiáis / ellos estudian.
Examples: Ella estudia mucho. / Nosotros estudiamos para el examen.
Pitfall: Third person singular -a looks like first person of some other patterns; confirm subject.

? When must you include the subject pronoun in Spanish?
Answer: Usually omitted because verb conjugation indicates subject; include only for emphasis or contrast.
Examples: ¿Estudias tú? Sí, yo estudio. / ¿Comen ellos? Ellos comen.
Pitfall: Overusing pronouns sounds unnatural; drop them unless needed.
