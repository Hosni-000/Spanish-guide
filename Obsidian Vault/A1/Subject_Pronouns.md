# A1 — Subject vs. Direct Object Pronouns (Basics)

:: When is the subject pronoun required? :: Almost never — only for emphasis or contrast.
Examples: ¿Tú estudias? / Yo estudio, tú descansas.
Pitfall: Do not start every sentence with yo/tú/él; it's unnatural.

? What are the direct object pronouns for singular and plural?
Answer: Me / te / lo / la / nos / os / los / las.
Examples: Ella me ayuda. / Nosotros los vemos.
Pitfall: Lo/la agree with noun gender/number; do not confuse with subject pronouns él/ella.

:: Give a full sentence using direct object pronoun replacement: "Veo el libro." -> "Lo veo." :: Yes; lo replaces el libro (masculine singular direct object).
Examples: Yo lo leo. / Nosotros la compramos.
Pitfall: Place pronoun BEFORE conjugated verb (or attached to infinitive/gerund at B1+).
