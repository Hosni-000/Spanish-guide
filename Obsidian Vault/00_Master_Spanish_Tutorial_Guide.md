# 00 — Master Spanish Tutorial Guide

> This guide explains how to use the vault. No HTML. No placeholders.

---

## 1. Learning Philosophy

Active recall beats passive reading. Every card in this vault uses the standard Obsidian Spaced Repetition format (`::` for single-line, `?` for multi-line). When you review cards, you retrieve the rule, not just recognize it. Combining CEFR progression (A1 → A2 → B1 → B2) with targeted verb drills (`#flashcards/drills`) creates a compound effect: grammar structures become automatic through vocabulary repetition, and vocabulary becomes durable through applied grammar context.

---

## 2. Obsidian Setup

### Required Plugin: Spaced Repetition
Search Obsidian Community Plugins for `Spaced Repetition` (author: Stepantsev Pavel). Install and enable.

### Plugin Settings (Configure in Settings > Spaced Repetition)
- Single-line card separator: `::`
- Multiline card separator: `?`
- Flashcard tags: `#flashcards`
- Drill tags: `#flashcards/drills`

---

## 3. Daily Workflow

1. Open the flashcard panel (Spaced Repetition: View flashcards).
2. Select deck `#flashcards` for vocabulary/grammar or `#flashcards/drills` for rapid conjugation speed drills.
3. Review cards by CEFR level. If a card is easy, rate it; if hard, rate it. The algorithm handles the rest.
4. After vocabulary cards, open one applied grammar file (`A1/Applied_Grammar_Vocab.md` etc.) to see the vocabulary used in sentence context.
5. End each session with a 5-minute drill session from `01_Verb_Drills/`.

## 5. Curriculum Roadmap

### Phase 1 — Foundation (A1)
- Present tense (regular -ar/-er/-ir + irregulars: ser, estar, ir, tener, hacer)
- Basic gender/article agreement (`el/la`, `un/una`)
- Essential nouns and high-frequency verbs
- Applied cards: gender agreement, present conjugation, ser vs. estar

### Phase 2 — Narrative Control (A2)
- Preterite vs. Imperfect distinction
- Reflexive routines (despertarse, vestirse, ducharse)
- Direct/indirect pronouns (`lo/la` / `se` rules)
- Applied cards: preterite contrast, reflexive placement, comparison structures

### Phase 3 — Mood & Nuance (B1)
- Present Subjunctive (formation + WEIRDO triggers)
- Imperfect Subjunctive (derivation + hypothetical clauses)
- Verbal periphrases (`llevar + gerundio`, `acabar de`, `dejar de`)
- Applied cards: subjunctive triggers with environment/work vocabulary, future/conditional, relative pronouns (`cuyo`)

### Phase 4 — Advanced Fluency (B2)
- Complex clause connections (`aunque`, `mientras`, `en cuanto` + subjunctive/indicative selection)
- Conditional Types 1/2/3
- Passive voice (`ser + participio` vs. pasiva refleja)
- Reported speech conversion rules
- Applied cards: complex clause selection, passive forms, formal register vocabulary

## 6. How to Progress Through Levels

- **A1:** Review all A1 files. Master `00_Spanish_Grammar_Index.md` (present + basic articles/nouns). Drill A1 vocabulary card by card.
- **A2:** Once A1 cards feel automatic (easy rating consistently), move to A2. Focus heavily on Preterite vs. Imperfect drills (`A2_Preterite_vs_Imperfect.md`) and vocabulary (`A2/Nouns_Daily_Life.md`).
- **B1:** Before starting B1 vocabulary, confirm you can use present and past tenses without checking rules. Read `B1_Present_Subjunctive_Triggers.md` and `B1_Imperfect_Subjunctive.md` before attempting `B1/Applied_Grammar_Vocab.md`.
- **B2:** Read `B2_Subjunctive_Indicative_Complex.md` first. Then use `B2/Nouns_Daily_Life.md` for formal vocabulary, integrating with `B2/Applied_Grammar_Vocab.md`.

## 7. Speed Drill Integration

Every session should include 5 minutes from `01_Verb_Drills/`:
- `01_Stem_Changing_Radicals.md`: Rapid conjugation recall (e→ie, o→ue, e→i).
- `02_Preterito_Indefinido_Irregulars.md`: Irregular stem groups (U/I/J/Totally irregular).
- `03_Subjunctive_Triggers_and_Forms.md`: Subjunctive forms linked directly to trigger words.

These drills are designed to automate conjugation patterns so vocabulary cards and applied grammar cards become faster over time.

---

## 8. Notes for Educators

- This vault does not contain full word list translations for every vocabulary file; it uses high-frequency representative cards per category. Expand by duplicating cards or adding your own.
- The structure separates pure vocabulary from applied grammar to prevent cognitive overload. Combine them only after the vocabulary base feels familiar.
- The vault is fully plain Markdown. You can open it in any Markdown editor or sync across devices with Obsidian Sync or Git.
