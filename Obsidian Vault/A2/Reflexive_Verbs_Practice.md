# A2 — Reflexive Verbs (Pronombres Reflexivos)

? How do reflexive verbs work at A2 level? :: Subject performs action on itself, so se is reflexive pronoun attached to verb or placed before conjugated verb; often means "to oneself."
Examples: Me cepillo los dientes. / Me visto a las seis.
Pitfall: Not all -arse/-es/-irse verbs are reflexive; check dictionary for se. Many everyday verbs (vestirse, despertarse, ducharse) reflexive.

:: Conjugate DORMIR (to sleep) fully, including reflexive form :: Yo duermo, tú duermes, él duerme, nosotros dormimos, vosotros dormís, ellos duermen. Reflexivos: Me duermo, te duermes, se duerme...
Examples: Me duermo temprano. / Vosotros os dormidis? Better: Nosotros dormimos bien.
Pitfall: Long-form reflexives stay close to verb (despertarse); short-form are made from stem + 3rd person personal pronoun + infinitives.

? What does "la + third person" mean in reflexive contexts?
Answer: Often does not translate literally; it expresses another person's action directed toward self — e.g., me lava la cara = he washes my face (action of third person toward me).
Examples: Se lava las manos? no: Me lavo las manos. / Él me lava el pelo.
Pitfall: This is advanced; solidify basics first with me, te, se(s), nos, os.
