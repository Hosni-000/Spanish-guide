# A2 — Applied Grammar + Vocabulary Cards

? Apply Preterite vs. Imperfect with routine/travel vocabulary: "When I was young, I traveled a lot; yesterday I went to the airport."?
Answer: Cuando era joven, viajaba mucho. Ayer fui al aeropuerto. | Grammar: imperfect (era, viajaba) for habitual past/description; preterite (fui) for completed action.

? Apply reflexive + health/body vocabulary with present: "Every morning I shower; my head hurts today; I feel tired."?
Answer: Cada mañana me ducho. Hoy me duele la cabeza. Me siento cansado. | Grammar: reflexive pronouns (me); present indicative; me duele indirect object structure; reflexive verb sentirse (e→i).

? Apply direct/indirect pronouns with shopping/travel vocabulary: "I bought the shirt; give the book to him; say it to me."?
Answer: Compré la camisa. Dale el libro a él. (or Dáselo). Dímelo. | Grammar: pronoun placement; direct pronoun agreement (la camisa → la); combined pronouns with se (indirect + direct).

? Apply comparison + travel/weather: "The flight is more expensive than the train; the weather is cloudier today."?
Answer: El vuelo es más caro que el tren. El tiempo está más nublado hoy. | Grammar: comparison (más + adj + que); adverb of comparison applied to weather description.
