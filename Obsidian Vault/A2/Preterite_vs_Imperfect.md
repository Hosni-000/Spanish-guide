# A2 — Preterite vs. Imperfect Contrast

:: When is Preterite used? :: Completed action at a specific time; sudden change; narrative events; action interrupted.
Examples: Ayer comí paella. / Se cayó de la escalera.
Pitfall: Preterite is for finished, bounded actions.

? When is Imperfect used?
Answer: Habitual/repeated actions; descriptions in past; background setting; emotional/mental states in the past; ongoing actions interrupted by preterite.
Examples: Cuando era niño, jugaba al fútbol. / El sol brillaba mientras caminaba.
Pitfall: Imperfect describes context and habits, not a single completed event.

:: How do you describe an interrupted action? :: Imperfect (ongoing background) interrupted by Preterite (interruption).
Examples: Yo leía cuando sonó el teléfono. / Estaba durmiendo cuando llegó mamá.
Pitfall: The interruption uses preterite; the interrupted action uses imperfect.
