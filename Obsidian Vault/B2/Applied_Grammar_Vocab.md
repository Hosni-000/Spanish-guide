# B2 — Applied Grammar + Vocabulary Cards (Formal / Complex)

? Apply B2 formal vocabulary + complex subjunctive/adverbial clauses: "Although the climate is changing, it's essential that governments adopt efficient measures."?
Answer: Aunque el clima esté cambiando, es esencial que los gobiernos adopten medidas eficientes. | Grammar: aunque + present subjunctive (esté cambiando — progressive in subjunctive; adoptén — present subjunctive of adoptar with stem change); es esencial que + subjunctive.

? Apply B2 politics/society vocabulary + conditional Type 2/3: "If I had been president, I would have implemented policies; I doubt it had been possible."?
Answer: Si hubiera sido presidente, habría implementado políticas. Dudo que hubiera sido posible. | Grammar: Type 3 conditional (pluperfect subjunctive + perfect conditional); dudo que + pluperfect subjunctive.

? Apply B2 abstract vocabulary + pasiva refleja/passive ser: "Evidence is presented by scientists; social networks are used to spread information."?
Answer: La evidencia es presentada por los científicos. Las redes sociales se utilizan para difundir información. / La información se difunde mediante las redes. | Grammar: ser + past participle (es presentada — gender/number agreement); pasiva refleja (se utilizan — third person plural to match redes sociales).
