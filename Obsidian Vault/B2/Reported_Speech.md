# B2 — Direct to Indirect Reported Speech Conversion Rules

:: How do you convert direct speech to indirect (reported) speech? :: Replace speaker's pronouns (yo→él/ella/usted), adjust for tense shift back: Presente → pretérito imperfecto / imperfecto; Imperfecto → preterite; Future → conditional or past future if shift beyond; Poder → Could have; Hablar → Talked/said (irregulars add e/i/j in affected tenses).
Examples: El profesor dijo: "Leeré el libro." → El profesor dijo que leería el libro. / Ella dijo que era estudiante.
Pitfall: Punctuation drops; include "que" after verbs of saying/spending/telling/asking/wondering etc.
