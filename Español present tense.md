# 🇪🇸 Spanish Present Tense

> The **presente de indicativo** is used for current actions, habits, general truths, some future events, and storytelling/narration.

---

## 1. When to Use the Present Tense

### 🔵 Current Actions
Actions happening **right now**.

> **Ahora hablo con Ana.**  
> I am speaking with Ana now.

### 🔁 Habitual Actions
Actions that happen regularly.

> **Como todas las mañanas.**  
> I eat every morning.

### 🌍 General Truths
Facts or statements generally or universally considered true.

> **El sol sale por el este.**  
> The sun rises in the east.

### 🔮 Near / Scheduled Future
Future actions can use the present when the context or time expression makes the future meaning clear.

> **Nos vemos mañana.**  
> See you tomorrow.

> **Mañana viajo a Madrid.**  
> I am traveling to Madrid tomorrow.

### 📖 Storytelling / Narration
The present can describe past events to make a story feel more immediate.

> **El héroe llega al castillo y rescata a la princesa.**  
> The hero arrives at the castle and rescues the princess.

### Common Time Expressions

- **ahora** — now
- **hoy** — today
- **cada día** — every day
- **normalmente** — normally
- **mañana** — tomorrow

# 2. Regular Present-Tense Conjugation

Take the infinitive and remove its ending:

- **-ar**
- **-er**
- **-ir**

Then add the appropriate ending.

---

## 🟦 -AR Verbs

Example: **hablar** — to speak

| Subject | Ending | hablar |
|---|---|---|
| yo | -o | **hablo** |
| tú | -as | **hablas** |
| él/ella/usted | -a | **habla** |
| nosotros/as | -amos | **hablamos** |
| vosotros/as | -áis | **habláis** |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes | -an | **hablan** |

### Pattern

**o — as — a — amos — áis — an**

---

## 🟩 -ER Verbs

Example: **comer** — to eat

| Subject | Ending | comer |
|---|---|---|
| yo | -o | **como** |
| tú | -es | **comes** |
| él/ella/usted | -e | **come** |
| nosotros/as | -emos | **comemos** |
| vosotros/as | -éis | **coméis** |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes | -en | **comen** |

### Pattern

**o — es — e — emos — éis — en**

---

## 🟨 -IR Verbs

Example: **vivir** — to live

| Subject | Ending | vivir |
|---|---|---|
| yo | -o | **vivo** |
| tú | -es | **vives** |
| él/ella/usted | -e | **vive** |
| nosotros/as | -imos | **vivimos** |
| vosotros/as | -ís | **vivís** |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes | -en | **viven** |

### Pattern

**o — es — e — imos — ís — en**

---

# 3. Subject Pronouns

Spanish often **omits the subject pronoun** because the verb ending identifies the subject.

> **Hablo español.**  
> = I speak Spanish.

You don't necessarily need **yo** because **-o** identifies the first-person singular form.

# 5. Stem-Changing Verbs

Also called **radical-changing verbs**.

The **stem changes** in the present tense.

The change usually happens in all forms **except nosotros and vosotros**.

### Main patterns

- **e → ie**
- **o → ue**
- **e → i**

---

## 🔵 e → ie

Example: **entender** — to understand

| Subject | Form |
|---|---|
| yo | **entiendo** |
| tú | **entiendes** |
| él/ella/usted | **entiende** |
| nosotros/as | **entendemos** |
| vosotros/as | **entendéis** |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes | **entienden** |

Notice:

> entiendo  
> entiendes  
> entiende  
> **entendemos** ← no change  
> **entendéis** ← no change  
> entienden

---

## 🟢 o → ue

Example: **poder** — to be able to

| Subject | Form |
|---|---|
| yo | **puedo** |
| tú | **puedes** |
| él/ella/usted | **puede** |
| nosotros/as | **podemos** |
| vosotros/as | **podéis** |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes | **pueden** |

---

## 🟡 e → i

Example: **pedir** — to ask for

| Subject | Form |
|---|---|
| yo | **pido** |
| tú | **pides** |
| él/ella/usted | **pide** |
| nosotros/as | **pedimos** |
| vosotros/as | **pedís** |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes | **piden** |

# 6. Reflexive Verbs

Reflexive verbs describe actions where the **subject and object are the same**.

In the present tense, put the reflexive pronoun **before the conjugated verb**.

### Reflexive Pronouns

| Subject | Pronoun |
|---|---|
| yo | **me** |
| tú | **te** |
| él/ella/usted | **se** |
| nosotros/as | **nos** |
| vosotros/as | **os** |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes | **se** |

### Example: levantarse — to get up

> **Me levanto temprano.**  
> I get up early.

> **Te levantas temprano.**  
> You get up early.

> **Se levanta temprano.**  
> He/she/you (formal) get up early.

> **Nos levantamos temprano.**  
> We get up early.

> **Os levantáis temprano.**  
> You all get up early.

> **Se levantan temprano.**  
> They/you all get up early.

### Other examples

**ducharse** — to shower

> **Me ducho por la mañana.**  
> I shower in the morning.

> **Nos duchamos por la mañana.**  
> We shower in the morning.

**vestirse** — to get dressed

> **Me visto rápidamente.**  
> I get dressed quickly.

> **Se visten rápidamente.**  
> They get dressed quickly.

The scan notes that **vestirse** is also a stem-changing verb: **e → i** in most forms.
