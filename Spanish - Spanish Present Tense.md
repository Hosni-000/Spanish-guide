# Spanish Present Tense

## 📌 Grammar Rule

The **presente de indicativo** is used for:

- actions happening now
- habitual actions
- general truths
- some near or scheduled future events
- storytelling/narration (historical present)

Spanish often omits the subject pronoun because the **verb ending identifies the subject**.

## 🧩 Structure

### Regular present tense

`infinitive - infinitive ending + present-tense ending`

Regular verbs follow separate patterns for `-ar`, `-er`, and `-ir` verbs.

### Reflexive verbs

`reflexive pronoun + conjugated verb`

Example: `Me levanto.`

### Stem-changing verbs

The source gives three main patterns:

- `e -> ie`
- `o -> ue`
- `e -> i`

The change usually occurs in all forms except **nosotros** and **vosotros**.

## 📝 Examples

### Uses of the present tense

- General truth: `El sol sale por el este.` — The sun rises in the east.
- Habitual action: `Estudio todas las noches.` — I study every night.
- Action happening now: `Ella come una manzana.` — She is eating an apple.
- Future action: `Mañana viajo a Madrid.` — I am traveling to Madrid tomorrow.
- Storytelling/narration: `El héroe llega al castillo y rescata a la princesa.` — The hero arrives at the castle and rescues the princess.

Common time expressions shown:

- `ahora` — now
- `hoy` — today
- `cada día` — every day
- `normalmente` — normally
- `mañana` — tomorrow

## ⚠️ Important Notes

### Regular endings

The source gives these regular present-tense endings and examples.

### Stem-changing verbs

The vowel change generally does not occur in the first- and second-person plural forms (`nosotros`, `vosotros`) in the source's examples.

### Irregular verbs

Irregular verbs do not follow the regular conjugation patterns in one or more forms. The source says their forms must be learned individually.

### Reflexive verbs

Reflexive pronouns in the present tense:

| Subject | Reflexive pronoun |
|---|---|
| yo | `me` |
| tú | `te` |
| él/ella/usted | `se` |
| nosotros/nosotras | `nos` |
| vosotros/vosotras | `os` |
| ellos/ellas/ustedes | `se` |

Example with `levantarse`:

- `Me levanto temprano.` — I get up early.
- `Te levantas temprano.` — You get up early.
- `Se levanta temprano.` — He, she, or you (formal) get up early.
- `Nos levantamos temprano.` — We get up early.
- `Os levantáis temprano.` — You all (informal) get up early.
- `Se levantan temprano.` — They or you all (formal) get up early.

Other reflexive verbs shown:

- `ducharse` — to shower
- `vestirse` — to get dressed

Examples:

- `Me ducho por la mañana.` — I shower in the morning.
- `Nos duchamos por la mañana.` — We shower in the morning.
- `Me visto rápidamente.` — I get dressed quickly.
- `Se visten rápidamente.` — They get dressed quickly.

> The source notes that `vestirse` is also stem-changing: `e -> i` in most forms.

## 💡 Remember

> ==Regular present tense = infinitive stem + the appropriate `-ar`, `-er`, or `-ir` ending.==

> In the source's stem-changing patterns, the change generally happens outside `nosotros` and `vosotros`.
