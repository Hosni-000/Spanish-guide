# Spanish Prepositions

## 📌 Grammar Rule

A **preposition** shows the relationship between nouns, pronouns, and other words in a sentence. In Spanish, prepositions express relationships of **time, place, direction, manner, and more**.

## 🧩 Structure

Common patterns include:

`preposition + noun/pronoun`

and verb-preposition combinations such as:

`verb + preposition`

Examples: `pensar en`, `soñar con`, `hablar de`, `depender de`, `preocuparse por`, `acordarse de`.

### Prepositions of place

- `en` — on, at: `La pluma está en la mesa.` — The pen is on the table.
- `sobre` — on top of: `El libro está sobre el escritorio.` — The book is on the desk.
- `debajo de` — under: `El gato está debajo de la cama.` — The cat is under the bed.
- `encima de` — on top of: `Hay una lámpara encima de la mesa.` — There is a lamp on top of the table.
- `detrás de` — behind: `El jardín está detrás de la casa.` — The garden is behind the house.
- `delante de` — in front of: `Hay un árbol delante de mi ventana.` — There is a tree in front of my window.
- `entre` — between: `El banco está entre la tienda y el restaurante.` — The bench is between the store and the restaurant.
- `cerca de` — near: `Vivo cerca del parque.` — I live near the park.
- `lejos de` — far from: `La playa está lejos de aquí.` — The beach is far from here.

### Prepositions of time and manner

**Time**

- `a` — at, for specific times: `La reunión es a las tres.` — The meeting is at three.
- `de` — from, during a period: `Trabajamos de nueve a cinco.` — We work from nine to five.
- `desde` — since, from a starting point: `Estoy esperando desde las dos.` — I have been waiting since two.
- `hasta` — until: `Estaré aquí hasta las seis.` — I'll be here until six.
- `durante` — during: `Durante el verano, voy a la playa.` — During the summer, I go to the beach.
- `en` — in, with months and years: `Nací en mayo.` — I was born in May.

**Manner**

- `con` — with, using: `Escribo con un lápiz.` — I write with a pencil.
- `sin` — without: `Prefiero el café sin azúcar.` — I prefer coffee without sugar.
- `por` — by, via: `Enviamos la carta por correo.` — We sent the letter by mail.

### De, en, and a

#### `de`

- Possession: `El libro de María.` — María's book.
- Origin: `Soy de México.` — I'm from Mexico.
- Material: `Una mesa de madera.` — A wooden table.
- Contents: `Una taza de café.` — A cup of coffee.
- Time: `Son las diez de la noche.` — It is ten at night.

#### `en`

- Location: `Estoy en casa.` — I'm at home.
- Time: `En verano hace calor.` — It is hot in summer.
- Transportation: `Viajo en tren.` — I travel by train.
- State: `Está en buena forma.` — He/She is in good shape.

#### `a`

- Direction: `Voy a la tienda.` — I'm going to the store.
- Time: `Llegamos a las ocho.` — We arrived at eight.
- Indirect object: `Le di el libro a Juan.` — I gave the book to Juan.
- Personal `a`: `Vi a mi amigo.` — I saw my friend.

### Verb + preposition patterns

- `Pensar en`: `Pienso en mis vacaciones.` — I think about my vacation.
- `Soñar con`: `Sueño con viajar por el mundo.` — I dream of traveling around the world.
- `Hablar de`: `Hablamos de política.` — We talked about politics.
- `Depender de`: `Dependo de mi trabajo.` — I depend on my job.
- `Preocuparse por`: `Me preocupo por ti.` — I worry about you.
- `Contar con`: `Puedes contar conmigo.` — You can count on me.
- `Acordarse de`: `Me acuerdo de ti.` — I remember you.

### Prepositional phrases

- `a causa de` — because of: `No pude venir a causa del tráfico.` — I couldn't come because of the traffic.
- `a pesar de` — despite: `A pesar de la lluvia, fuimos al parque.` — Despite the rain, we went to the park.
- `acerca de` — about: `Hablamos acerca de su nuevo trabajo.` — We talked about their new job.
- `al lado de` — next to: `Mi casa está al lado del parque.` — My house is next to the park.
- `antes de` — before: `Siempre me ducho antes de desayunar.` — I always shower before having breakfast.
- `después de` — after: `Después de la clase, vamos a comer.` — After class, we're going to eat.
- `en vez de` — instead of: `Tomé té en vez de café.` — I had tea instead of coffee.
- `por medio de` — by means of: `Lo arreglamos por medio de negociaciones.` — We resolved it through negotiations.

## ⚠️ Important Notes

- The source recommends learning prepositions **in context with verbs or phrases** because each verb may take a particular preposition.
- Compare `Voy a la escuela.` with the source's incorrect example `Voy en la escuela.` when expressing going to school.
- `Soy de España.` expresses origin; the source contrasts it with `Vengo desde España.` for movement from a starting point.

## 🔄 Conjugation / Forms

Prepositions do not conjugate. The important forms in this lesson are the fixed verb + preposition combinations shown above.

## 💡 Remember

> Learn Spanish prepositions as part of **complete phrases**, especially with verbs.
