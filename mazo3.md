10. 🧠 Grammar Flashcards

These are worth having alongside the vocabulary cards because they force you to recall the system, not just individual words.

What are the regular present-tense endings for -AR verbs?:::o, as, a, amos, áis, an

What are the regular present-tense endings for -ER verbs?:::o, es, e, emos, éis, en

What are the regular present-tense endings for -IR verbs?:::o, es, e, imos, ís, en

What are the three main present-tense stem changes?:::e → ie, o → ue, e → i

Which subject forms normally do NOT undergo a stem change?:::nosotros/as and vosotros/as

What are the Spanish reflexive pronouns?:::me, te, se, nos, os, se

Where does the reflexive pronoun normally go in the present tense?:::Before the conjugated verb

🔄 Production Cards

These are slightly harder because they make you produce the Spanish, not just recognize it.

“I study every day.”:::Estudio cada día.

“I get up early.”:::Me levanto temprano.

“I have a lot of work.”:::Tengo mucho trabajo.

“We live in the city.”:::Vivimos en la ciudad.

“They understand Spanish.”:::Entienden español.

“Can you speak more slowly?”:::¿Puedes hablar más despacio?

“I want to learn Spanish.”:::Quiero aprender español.

“She gets dressed quickly.”:::Se viste rápidamente.

“We go to university.”:::Vamos a la universidad.

“They sleep at night.”:::Duermen por la noche.
